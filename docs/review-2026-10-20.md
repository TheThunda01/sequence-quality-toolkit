# Scientific and editorial review — 20 October 2026

This review checked the authored guides, example reports, provenance/search records, and relevant implementation. Source rechecks finished on 21 October 2026 UTC. The file contracts and scientific calculations remain unchanged.

## Corrections and clarifications

- Added a consistent [bibliography](references.md) for the nine adopted papers and the official data, format, and software references scattered across the guides. Completed DOI/PMID, year, issue, and page/article information where verified.
- Corrected a link labeled “RNA structure” to the actual textbook chapter, *From DNA to RNA*. Its present access limitation is recorded rather than claiming that its full text was reopened.
- Clarified that the paired-file parser uses its own documented grammar. The Illumina page informs that grammar but has different character and sample-field restrictions; accepting a name does not prove biological pairing.
- Defined the Phred equation with an accessible official source and explicitly distinguished per-base probabilities from an error-free-read claim. The vendor page's broad wording about perfect reads is not adopted.
- Explained that summing quality-implied error probabilities uses linearity of expectation and does not itself require independent errors. Interpreting the result still depends on score calibration, which this project does not measure.
- Corrected obsolete “future integrated preflight” wording: the input-lock check is already implemented. Earlier search logs and implementation records remain unchanged.
- Preserved the public example as the first 1,000 archived single-end yeast reads, a convenience prefix. It does not represent the whole run, and the paired examples remain synthetic.

## Assumptions and vocabulary

The [assumption register](assumptions.md) explains the DNA alphabet restriction, quality encoding, score calibration, weighted means, cycle denominators, GC convention, grouping, paired names, prefix selection, archive labels, byte locks, and resource/failure limits. Biological identity, representativeness, and calibration are not established by the file checks.

The [glossary](glossary.md) defines the relevant biological, statistical, and computing vocabulary. First-use explanations were added to the README, methods, dictionaries, and practical guide, including FASTA/FASTQ, GC, N50, Phred+33, JSON Lines, SHA-256, and unique molecular identifiers.

## Source coverage and limits

The [machine-readable audit](reference-audit.json) contains 26 distinct adopted sources: nine article records and 17 official resources. Eight articles were rechecked at abstract level; the associated Schurch erratum has metadata but no abstract. Thirteen official resources were accessible. Two textbook chapters and the live run/project metadata could not be reopened in this pass.

The original source accession, archived provenance, subset hashes, and prior download-check record are preserved. The complete approximately 60 MB run was not downloaded again. Earlier full-text appraisal of Cock et al. remains historical and is not represented as repeated. Source access and partial appraisal limit the review; it is not an exhaustive systematic review, external-tool benchmark, or biological validation.
