## Source and interpretation checks

The [official ENA file-report API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/filereport?accession=ERR458493&result=read_run&fields=run_accession,study_accession,scientific_name,library_layout,library_strategy,fastq_md5&format=tsv) identifies ERR458493 as a single-end RNA-seq run from *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* in PRJEB5348. Its listed full-file MD5 still matches the provenance record. The full FASTQ was not downloaded again; this does not supply a fresh whole-file verification.

The [Illumina quality-score definition](https://www.illumina.com/science/technology/next-generation-sequencing/plan-experiments/quality-scores.html) was checked against the project's equations. Q30 implies an estimated per-base error probability of 0.001. It does not guarantee an error-free read. Expected-error sums, arithmetic mean Q, score-threshold fractions, and observed mismatch counts remain distinct concepts in the methods.

The README, biology/concepts, FASTA and FASTQ methods, paired-file contract, dictionaries, glossary, assumptions, and practical guide were reviewed for consistent terminology and limits. GC excludes ambiguity symbols from its stated denominator; N50 describes the supplied lengths; cycle statistics include only reads reaching that cycle. The equations and GitHub math delimiters are consistent with these definitions. A grammar correction clarifies the ASCII-to-score mapping without changing it.
